NVDA Audio Processor - localization rule (version 1.0)

English is the source language.

IMPORTANT: DSP/effect/parameter terminology follows a VST-style convention and must remain in standard English in every locale. Pure technical labels are intentionally not included in nvda.pot.

Examples that must remain English:
Low Pass, High Pass, Band 1 Frequency, Gain, Q, Master Gain, 1176 Compressor, Input, Output, Attack, Release, Ratio, Pan, Pan LFO, Waveform, Rate, Smoothing, Depth, Stereo Chorus, Chorus Mode, Width, Mix, Stereo Delay, Time, Feedback, Send, Ping-Pong, Stereo Reverb, Size, Decay, Damping, Limiter, Ceiling, Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, S&H.

Factory preset names are also kept in English, as in audio plug-ins. The surrounding preset-management interface and messages are localized.

Translate the surrounding NVDA interaction language, messages, states, errors and action descriptions.

Examples:
English source: Enable or disable Stereo Chorus.
Italian: Attiva o disattiva Stereo Chorus.

English source: Increase Low Pass Frequency.
Italian: Aumenta Low Pass Frequency.

English source: Preset saved: {name}
Italian: Preset salvato: {name}

Do not alter internal JSON values or identifiers.

Locales included in version 1.0:
- en: English source/fallback
- it: Italian
- es: Spanish
- fr: French

Documentation is included in doc/en, doc/it, doc/es and doc/fr.
The Spanish, French and Italian catalogs and documents follow the same rule: technical DSP terms embedded in translated sentences are preserved in English.
